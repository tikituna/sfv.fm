San Fernando Valley Radio is live from Berry Drive whenever Sam is on the air. The television has three channels. Channel 2 is a California Department of Transportation camera on a Valley freeway: live video where your browser can reach it, a still refreshed every minute where it cannot. Between the cameras come the Valley weather and old Valley commercials, from recordings kept by the Internet Archive. Channel 4 is Huell Howser, by way of the Huell Howser Archives at Chapman University, which keeps his programs and the player they play in. Channel 7 is the Monterey Bay Aquarium, live. Press 2, 4 or 7.
